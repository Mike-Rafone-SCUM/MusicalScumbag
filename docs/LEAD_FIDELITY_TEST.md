# Lead Fidelity audition (3.9.5)

Choose Custom performance, then Range strategy → Lead Fidelity. Keep your
instrument, tracks, transpose, speed, density and Fluid settings the same for
each comparison. Use Compare to inspect Harmonic, Melody Contour and Lead
Fidelity, then prepare the same short vocal passage and instrumental bridge.

Lead Fidelity minimizes traced lead octave displacement first, ensemble pitch
displacement second, lead interval distortion third, and bank travel last.
Held traced leads remain protected during intervening backing attacks. Where
the arrangement provides no lead, the strongest attack is the fallback.
Only whole-octave folding occurs; no harmony or scale correction is generated.

Listen for vocal register, phrase continuity, backing balance and clipped
attacks. Include a passage crossing registers and a sustained singer over
offbeat backing. Record the MIDI, selected tracks, arrangement and settings
with your verdict. A poor source transcription remains a poor source.

SCUM shares one octave bank across a chord. Backing can fold to protect the
lead, and the existing switch budget can fold even lead notes. Timing guards
can shorten holds or delay attacks. Compare reports these tradeoffs; retained
note counts do not measure musical accuracy. Playback remains supervised with
focus protection and F8 stop.

This release has no current-build SCUM musical validation or new representative
MP3/WAV/M4A Basic Pitch validation. Optional inference remains isolated.
